@target 3
@names Bug

---
@pic 🐞🪵☀️

---
@pic 🐞🚌

---
@pic 🚌

---
@pic 🐱🐶🐔

---
@pic 💥

---
@pic 🐞☕

---
@pic 🐔😲

@pic 🐞🤢

---
@pic 🐞🪵
Bug gets off the bus. Bug sits on his log. "A bus? Not for me!" said Bug. "A log is the best!"
