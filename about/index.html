About

Hi, I'm Geoff.

I build AI-powered investment research tools at Arch Labs. Before that: chat design at LivePerson, experimentation tooling at Agoda, community and quality systems at Answers.com, and a decade of building systems at HBO.

Currently: building a challenging puzzle game called Lightyard · writing a sequel to my recent novella · prototyping an educational reading app.

What I do

I build things from 0-1: discovery and ideation, prototyping, iteration, testing with customers, release and marketing.

Most recently I shipped Arch Investment Research. It uses AI to analyze documents, extract facts, and flag caution points worth a closer look. Agents power its chat and its deep research across the web, and it generates reports, one-pagers, and decision memos. I helped decide to build it, did the client research, built the prototype, ran the customer pilots, turned the feedback into priorities, and worked with marketing and sales on pricing and promotion.

I partner with developers — I’d rather brainstorm and discuss than dictate.

AI is key to what I do at every stage: AI powers the tools I build, and AI is a key component of how I build them.
