Lightyard

Lightyard is a challenging puzzle game I created where you set up mirrors to get packets of light from the emitters to the receivers.

Lightyard is a puzzle game inspired by Trainyard, which I loved and which is no longer in the App Store. Instead of laying track for trains, you place mirrors, half-mirrors, and prisms to steer packets of colored light from emitters to receivers. Each receiver wants a specific sequence of colors, arriving from a specific side, in order.

Mixing light

When two packets meet, they merge and take on the union of their colors: red and blue make magenta, red and green make yellow, green and blue make cyan, and anything that meets white becomes white. A prism can split a packet back apart, so magenta becomes blue and red. Add crystals that slow or filter light, transformers that step a color along the spectrum, and optional rules like No Interactions and Strict Count, and things get interesting.

Build your own

Lightyard comes with many puzzles built in, and you can create your own and share them: puzzles and solutions can be copied, exported, and imported by anyone else with the app. It’s a single self-contained web app, and everything you create is saved in your browser.

I created it using Claude and ChatGPT.
